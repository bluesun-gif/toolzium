About Toolzium

Simplifying daily web utility workflows safely and instantly.

Welcome to Toolzium, a free platform offering over 50+ simple web-based utilities for designers, developers, students, and professionals alike.

Our Mission

Most websites ask you to upload your sensitive PDFs, documents, or data to their remote servers to convert them. This poses severe data privacy risks. Our mission is to build high-quality, professional tools that process 100% locally in your browser using WebAssembly, JavaScript, and modern HTML5 APIs. Your data never leaves your computer.

What We Offer

  • PDF Tools: Merge, split, compress, and rotate files.
  • Text Tools: Counters, case converters, fancy styles, and lorem generators.
  • Developer Utilities: JSON formatters, base64 encoders, and live viewer tools.
  • Converters & Calculators: Tip, loan, BMI, age, and temperature utilities.
